Flagship Financial Inclusion

IMBEJU STREET UNIVERSITY

Taking practical financial literacy, startup incubation, and capital linkages directly to youth and informal traders in communities.

Established to address the acute lack of formal financial education and credit readiness among youth, the Imbeju project conducts intensive bootcamps, equips entrepreneurs with bookkeeping tools, and connects viable business proposals with financial institutions and micro-grant facilities.

35,000+ Youth Trained in Financial Discipline
4,200+ Micro-Enterprises Linked to Banking

Core Project Activities:

  • Practical 3-day community financial bootcamps
  • Direct mentoring on cash flow, pricing, and savings
  • Partnership with banks and youth funds for seed capital
  • Ongoing follow-up clinics for enterprise sustainability
Fourth Industrial Revolution

AI & TECHNOLOGY PROGRAMS

Democratizing artificial intelligence literacy, digital productivity workflows, and remote tech work for African youth.

This initiative bridges the digital divide by demystifying AI and showing university students, freelancers, and innovators how to harness generative AI tools, write effective prompts, build software prototypes, and access global remote employment from Tanzania.

18,000+ Youth Introduced to AI Tools
25+ Campuses University Labs & Tech Hubs

Key Curriculum Tracks:

  • Practical AI prompt engineering and automation for business
  • Remote freelancing on global marketplaces
  • Digital content creation, editing & audience monetization
  • Problem-solving hackathons for local challenges

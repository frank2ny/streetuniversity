About Street University

Taking Knowledge Closer to the People

Street University is an initiative focused on creating accessible platforms where young people and communities can learn, connect, discover opportunities, and develop their true potential.

Democratizing Education Beyond Classrooms

Since its establishment in 2008 by Global Publishers, the initiative has continued to create spaces for dialogue, knowledge sharing, entrepreneurship, leadership, technology, innovation, personal development, and economic empowerment.

Our work brings together young people, seasoned professionals, entrepreneurs, educators, technology experts, and institutions to exchange knowledge and create tangible economic opportunities.

"Our approach is practical: we do not only talk about change; we create platforms through which people can gain knowledge, develop skills, connect with others, and take action."

Street University also provides a platform through which diverse programs and social movements can be developed and implemented in response to emerging economic and community needs.

OUR VISION

To become a leading platform for youth empowerment, knowledge sharing, innovation, and opportunity creation in Tanzania and beyond.

OUR MISSION

To create accessible platforms that equip young people and communities with relevant knowledge, practical skills, networks, and opportunities that enable them to discover their potential, pursue meaningful livelihoods, and contribute positively to society.

WHY STREET UNIVERSITY?

Because Potential Needs Opportunity

Young people possess enormous potential to contribute to social and economic development. However, potential alone is not always enough. Access to relevant knowledge, practical skills, mentorship, networks, and capital makes the critical difference.

OUR JOURNEY SINCE 2008

17+ Years of Youth Empowerment

Street University’s journey began in 2008 with a simple yet radical idea: knowledge should be accessible to people wherever they are. Founded by Global Publishers, the initiative has continuously evolved to empower generation after generation.

2008 Street University Established
17+ Years of Continuous Impact
TANZANIA Growing National Reach
2008 — The Genesis

Founded by Global Publishers

Street University was born from the realization that millions of aspiring youth needed practical business and life knowledge beyond traditional lecture halls.

2012–2016 — Campus & High School Tours

University & College Mobilization

Expanded across higher learning institutions across Dar es Salaam, Morogoro, and Arusha, inspiring students to embrace self-employment and entrepreneurial thinking.

2017–2021 — National Mega Conferences

Broadcasts & Public Community Forums

Scaled to thousands-capacity auditoriums like Mlimani City and open-air municipal squares, broadcast nationwide through Global TV Online.

2022–2024 — Imbeju & Financial Partnerships

Institutional Economic Empowerment

Forged strategic collaborations with financial institutions and banks to transition youth from motivation to tangible capital access and micro-enterprise growth.

2025–Present — AI & Future Skills

Digital Transformation & Pan-African Vision

Integrating artificial intelligence, digital productivity tools, and cross-border innovation networks, taking knowledge to every corner of Tanzania and beyond.
